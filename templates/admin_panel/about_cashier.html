School Cashier & Directory Services

The central treasury operations department of the institution, responsible for student accounting, tuition collection ledger updates, supplier cheque disbursements, audit compliance, and cash management. Systems are protected via multi-factor biometrics.

Cashiering Services & Core Operations

Tuition & Fees Management

Processes tuition fee payments, laboratory fees, miscellaneous fees, and manages student ledger cards, installment arrangements, and examination clearances.

Cheque Disbursements

Prepares, records, and releases checks to suppliers, employees, contractors, and other third-party payees, cross-referencing disbursements against UACS budget clusters.

Tax Withholding & Divisors

Automatically calculates VAT (using the 1.06 divisor format), Professional Fees withholding taxes, and EWT deductions to ensure compliance with national revenue rules.

Vault & Vault Custody

Responsible for balancing cash drawer registers daily, storing safe cash reserves, and prepping daily bank deposits via secure armored transport.

Petty Cash Custody

Maintains and replenishes petty cash funds for quick administrative and departmental expense requisitions, managing vouchers and receipts audits.

Refunds & Credit Adjustments

Issues credit memos and handles student refunds for dropped courses, overpayments, scholar grants, and formal student withdrawals.

Biometric MFA Enforcement

Protects transaction integrity by requiring three levels of verification (RFID badge tapping, fingerprint scans, and facial verification) for administrative ledger entries.

RCI Registry Reporting

Compiles and formats structured disbursements journals into Report of Checks Issued (RCI) reports, exporting printable PDF summaries for auditors.
